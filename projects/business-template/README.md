# ApexGrowth — Corporate & Agency Business Template

A modern, high-converting agency and B2B corporate template engineered with semantic HTML5, modern CSS Grid/Flexbox, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Features
- **Hero & Analytics Dashboard**: Clean visual mockup demonstrating business performance metrics and growth indicators.
- **Services Grid**: Highlight 3 to 6 corporate solutions with feature checkmarks.
- **Dynamic Pricing Calculator**: Interactive monthly vs. annual pricing toggle with a 20% discount calculation.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion**: Smooth expand/collapse question cards to resolve customer objections.
- **Lead Generation Form**: Quick discovery call reservation form with instant feedback.
- **Mobile First & Lightweight**: Zero external frameworks, fast initial load, responsive at all viewports.

## Technologies Used
- HTML5 (Semantic sections, accessible form labels)
- CSS3 (Custom properties, grid layouts, animations, transitions)
- JavaScript (Toggle switch, accordion logic, form handling)

## How to Customize for a Client
1. Change agency branding in `index.html` (search for `ApexGrowth`).
2. Adjust monthly and annual rates in `<span class="amount" data-monthly="499" data-annual="399">`.
3. Add or edit frequently asked questions inside `<div class="accordion">`.
4. Connect the form submission in `script.js` to an email endpoint (e.g. Formspree or EmailJS).
